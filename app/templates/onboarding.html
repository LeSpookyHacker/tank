Before we begin.

Tank is a local-first knowledge base and AI partner for new security engineers. You feed it your employer's docs — architecture, CMDB, IAM policies, Sigma rules, org charts — and it builds a redacted knowledge graph your team never sees. Chat, threat models, reports, and your 90-day plan all run against that graph.

The privacy contract, before anything else:

  • Nothing leaves this machine in cleartext.
  • Internal hostnames, emails, IPs, AWS account IDs, and secrets are redacted locally before any Claude API call.
  • Secrets are one-way hashed — Tank cannot rehydrate them.
  • Wipe everything with one button in Settings.

Step 1 — Your role

This sets the framing Tank uses across chat, reports, and your daily brief.

Step 2 — Internal TLD (optional)

The suffix shared by all your internal hostnames. If services live at api.corp.internal, your TLD is corp.internal. Every hostname ending in this domain is replaced with INTERNAL_HOST_* before any data reaches the AI. Leave blank if you're not sure — you can set it in Settings later.

The intake interview (next step) is a 20-question structured interview that seeds your knowledge graph and generates your Day-1 brief. It takes about five minutes and is the fastest way to make Tank useful.
